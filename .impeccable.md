## Design Context

### Users
Nontechnical SMM users blocked on exporting a report, and founders reviewing diagnostic evidence. Primary context is a live presentation in two days. User explicitly requested a Fin-like embedded support experience.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, helpful. Visual direction is an implementation assumption grounded in the requested embedded SaaS experience; adjustable without changing the flow.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light operational workspace, warm off-white canvas, deep pine accent, restrained typography and tables, clear support panel. Chinese product copy. Keep technical evidence behind a developer view; show honest runtime status rather than decorative activity.

### Design Principles
1. Open on the working report surface, not a landing page.
2. Preserve context and identity; never ask users to repeat a login in support.
3. Give failed, pending, unavailable and completed states distinct truthful copy.
4. Make the support experience readable on a projector and a small screen.
5. Use real evidence and runtime events; never fabricate diagnosis progress.
